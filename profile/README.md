# GROWL: Gravitational Wave Paleontology Lab

We study the populations of merging black holes and neutron stars observed in gravitational waves, and what they reveal about how massive binary stars live and die. Based at UC San Diego.

## Projects

- **[growl-catalog](https://github.com/growl-lab/growl-catalog)**: a standardized public catalog of compact-binary population simulations, with data access and physics assumptions documented for each.

## Joining

Lab members join this organization after completing the GW Paleontology Bootcamp. Discussion happens on the GW Paleontology Discord.

## Open science

Our code and data are public by default. If you use them, please cite the relevant Zenodo release.
